## 1. Mission and anti-drift decision

Make exactly ONE evidence-first bridge from Seven-Voices spoken structure to independently testable candidate sound/function relationships. Study multiple Albanian linguistic traditions, define falsifiable rules *before examining a confirmatory outcome*, evaluate a bounded corpus with negative controls and provenance, and record support, contradiction, confounding or Null. Deliver one auditable report and close this milestone only against the Definition of Done below.

Do NOT restart an endless word-by-word retrospective exercise, count infrastructure PRs as discoveries, or silently replace the existing frozen M2 procedure.

### Research posture

- Canonical Voices: A, E, I, O, U, Y, Ë. Y is a vowel. IPA precedes orthography.
- Spoken vowels form nuclei; consonants constrain their context, but do not automatically create Ë just because an Albanian consonant letter name contains Ë.
- The observed source consonant order is not, by itself, a phonological syllabification or a semantic operator.
- Preserve Fact / Inference / Hypothesis / Unknown or Null, provenance/evidenceRefs, dialect, variation, no_single_winner and user_decides.
- User propositions such as I = light/visibility/knowing and Ë = mother/feminine are *hypotheses*, NOT frozen truth; symbolic colors are not empirical pigment predictions.
- Conventional etymology, alternative symbolic interpretation, phonetic resemblance and testable semantic prediction answer different questions. No scholar has veto power; no author is automatically proved correct.

### Phase 3 — Predeclare exactly three hypothesis families

H-I: For explicitly qualified spoken /i/ or reviewed language-specific I-Voice equivalents, is the independently defined light/visibility/knowing domain enriched relative to matched baselines?

H-Ë: For actually realized Albanian Ë nuclei (not written Ë, silent final Ë or unqualified schwa), is an explicitly defined mother/maternal/feminine domain enriched relative to matched controls?

H-DI: Treat contiguous spoken /d i t/, /d ɾ i t/ and /d i/ as SEPARATE candidate structural rules, with different, predeclared predicted functions. Do not slide from one to another after seeing meanings. Read Zheji's actual rule separately.

Predeclare ALL of these before the evaluation outcome: IPA normalization and language/dialect profiles; entry/lexical-root family unit; morphology and cognate deduplication; borrowed/native strata based on independent etymological evidence; inclusion/exclusion and unavailable-source policy; semantic labels, positive and negative poles (light vs absence of light as distinct tests); baseline/null model, confounds, controls, holdout, analysis/uncertainty measure and stopping rule. **Exit:** One versioned, prospective EXPLORATORY protocol recorded before outcome inspection. If all relevant meanings were already exposed in pilot examples, mark those examples exploratory and identify unused held-out material; no retroactive confirmatory classification.
