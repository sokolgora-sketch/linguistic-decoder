## 2. ZË-RO doctrine

The project doctrine is:

1. Language is not arbitrary in the Saussurean sense.
2. Sound and meaning are connected at a deep structural level.
3. Petro Zheji is the intellectual and research lineage identified by DF for
   this position.
4. ZË-RO / Open Instrument continues this lineage as its own research and
   implementation program.
5. The seven canonical Voices/vowels are `A`, `E`, `I`, `O`, `U`, `Y`, and
   `Ë`.
6. The seven canonical vowels carry the nucleus.
7. Consonants make the shades.
8. Word forms are treated as structurally motivated rather than as random
   sound-meaning pairings.
9. Albanian is treated within this doctrine as preserving motivated internal
   architecture in which consonants carry foundational meaning and vowels
   organize and modulate it.
10. ZË-RO investigates this structure through explicit, deterministic,
    evidence-traceable mechanisms rather than treating the doctrine itself as
    something that every bounded experiment must re-decide.

These are statements of `USER_DOCTRINE`, not claims that a bounded experiment
has already established every mechanism implied by them.

## 4. Terminology: the “arbitrary” collision

English `arbitrary` creates a terminology collision between unrelated
technical/computing and linguistic usages.

### 4.1 Technical and computing usage

Historical frozen artifacts may use the phrase `arbitrary-precision
arithmetic`. That wording remains historical truth and is not retroactively
rewritten by this record.

For new Open Instrument / ZË-RO prose, prefer `unbounded-precision` or, where
technically more exact, `variable-width integer`, `variable-width count
representation`, `unbounded-precision integer`, or `unbounded-precision count
representation`.

### 4.2 Linguistic usage

Do not use standalone `arbitrary` as though Saussurean arbitrariness were a
settled project premise. When the opposing linguistic position must be
named, use an explicit term such as `Saussurean arbitrariness`,
`Saussurean arbitrary-sign position`, or `unmotivated-sign position`, as
appropriate to the context.

### 4.3 Homonym rule

Technical terms such as `unbounded-precision bigint counts` describe a
computational representation. They say nothing about whether word forms are
structurally motivated. No inference may be made between the technical and
linguistic usages merely because both are sometimes described with
`arbitrary`.

## 5. Working rule for future lanes

The project operates within DF's doctrine. Experiments test bounded
mechanisms inside it. Do not frame the doctrine itself as an open question.
Do not default to Saussurean assumptions when reasoning about word structure.

Operationally, a bounded experiment may return `SUPPORTED`, `UNSUPPORTED`,
`NULL`, `INVALID`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, or the exact equivalent defined by
its governing contract. That result applies to the particular bounded
proposition or mechanism tested. It does not automatically become a verdict
on the entire ZË-RO doctrine unless an explicitly authorized experiment was
designed to test that broader proposition.

For example, a bounded consonantal-interaction experiment returning `NULL`
means that the specified interaction was not established under that
experiment. It does not mean that language is arbitrary, consonants have no
function, the doctrine failed, or word forms are unmotivated. Conversely, a
positive result for one bounded mechanism does not prove the entire doctrine.

Preserve the distinction between `Fact`, `Inference`, `Hypothesis`, and
`Unknown / Null`. Do not manufacture evidence, reinterpret failed controls,
alter thresholds, or overstate results in order to protect doctrine. Doctrine
determines the research frame; evidence determines which bounded empirical
claims have actually been established.

## 8. Seven-Voices core boundary

The canonical Seven Voices are exactly:

`A`, `E`, `I`, `O`, `U`, `Y`, `Ë`.

`Y` remains a canonical Voice unless an explicitly defined transliteration
mode says otherwise. Vowels drive the Seven-Voices analysis/path. Consonants
shape, configure, and make shades, but do not independently become new
canonical Voices.

This record creates no eighth Voice and no canonical individual-consonant
semantic map. `Consonants make the shades` is doctrine; the exact
computational or empirical mechanism by which consonants produce those shades
remains subject to bounded contracts and evidence.

proof, candidate evidence, or production evidence. Likewise, absence of
