# Open Instrument — NDAJ / NDARË DA-Family Derivative Support Contract v0.1

## Status
Locked contract baseline.

## Purpose

This document locks the current repo posture for **NDAJ / NDARË** inside Open Instrument.

The goal is to prevent drift.

NDAJ / NDARË are currently allowed as **DA-family derivative support**.

They are **not** currently allowed as standalone reviewed-source evidence.
They are **not** currently promoted as standalone reviewed-runtime evidence.
They do **not** validate DA by themselves.

---

## Current accepted posture

NDAJ / NDARË currently belong to the **DA-family derivative-support boundary**.

That means they may appear as:

- derivative family support in semantic/operator profiles
- dialect/carrier evidence in DA-family tests
- supportive divide/share/separate family evidence

That does **not** mean they may appear as:

- isolated reviewed-source closure for DA
- standalone reviewed operator promotion
- reviewed runtime projection by themselves
- substitute evidence for exact isolated reviewed `da`

---

## What is allowed

### 1. Semantic family support
NDAJ / NDARË may reinforce the semantic neighborhood around DA:

- split
- divide
- separate
- share
- divided

### 2. Carrier / dialect support
NDAJ / NDARË may appear in DA-family carrier or dialect-support tests and notes.

### 3. Derivative-family wording
Repo docs/tests may explicitly describe NDAJ / NDARË as:

- DA-family derivative support
- derivative family support
- supportive family evidence

---

## What is not allowed

### 1. Not standalone reviewed-source evidence
NDAJ / NDARË are not currently valid reviewed-source closure for DA.

### 2. Not standalone operator promotion
NDAJ / NDARË are not currently promoted as standalone reviewed operators.

### 3. Not runtime reviewed projection
NDAJ / NDARË do not currently project as their own reviewed-runtime evidence.

### 4. Not replacement for exact isolated `da`
Derivative evidence must not replace exact isolated reviewed-source evidence for `da`.

---

## Boundary rule

The repo currently distinguishes between:

### Stronger evidence
Exact isolated reviewed-source evidence for the operator itself.

### Weaker evidence
Derivative family support, morphology support, dialect support, and carrier support.

NDAJ / NDARË currently remain in the weaker class.

---

## Exact contract decision

The repo should preserve all of the following:

1. `ndaj` and `ndarë` may remain visible as DA-family derivative support
2. they may remain visible in dialect/carrier support
3. they may reinforce DA-family semantics
4. they may **not** validate DA by themselves
5. they may **not** silently become standalone reviewed-source evidence
6. they may **not** silently become standalone reviewed-runtime evidence

---

## Smallest future widening lane

If NDAJ is widened later, that must happen in a separate reviewed-source lane.

That future lane would need to answer:

- whether NDAJ gets its own reviewed source row
- whether NDAJ remains only DA-family derivative support
- whether NDAJ ever becomes standalone reviewed-runtime evidence

This contract does **not** approve that widening.
