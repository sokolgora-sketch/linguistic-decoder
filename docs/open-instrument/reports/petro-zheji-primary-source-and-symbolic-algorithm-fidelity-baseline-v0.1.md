## Milestone decision

Open the Petro Zheji Primary Source and Symbolic Algorithm Fidelity Baseline v0.1 milestone.

This milestone exists because the repository contains extensive Zheji-named code, documents, tests, prompts, artifacts, replay reports, UI overlays, and operator work, but it does not yet contain a verified page-cited primary-source corpus proving that those components faithfully implement Petro Zheji's documented philosophy or complete Symbolic Algorithm.

The milestone must determine what is:

- directly documented by Petro Zheji;
- reconstructed from Petro Zheji sources;
- merely inspired by Petro Zheji;
- original to ZË-RO Seven Voices;
- original to Open Instrument engineering;
- produced by a model or replay;
- conventional historical-linguistics context;
- unresolved or unknown.

The milestone does not begin by implementing more runtime behavior.

It begins by establishing source fidelity.

## Authoritative-source hierarchy

Sources must be evaluated in this order.

### Level 1 — Petro Zheji primary works

Preferred evidence:

- authenticated published editions;
- page-stable book scans;
- Petro Zheji manuscripts or articles with stable provenance;
- exact page-cited passages.

### Level 2 — Edition and publication records

Supporting evidence:

- publisher records;
- national or university library catalogues;
- ISBN and edition metadata;
- tables of contents;
- publication dates;
- pagination and volume identity.

### Level 3 — Serious secondary analysis

Supporting context:

- scholarly studies;
- critical editions;
- academically attributable interpretation;
- interviews with direct source quotations.

Secondary analysis must not replace a primary definition when the primary work is available.

### Level 4 — General web commentary

General commentary may assist discovery.

It may not establish a direct implementation claim by itself.

### Level 5 — Open Instrument internal material

Internal documents, prompts, artifacts, code, tests, replays, and DF_BRAIN history describe this project's work.

They do not define Petro Zheji's original philosophy.
