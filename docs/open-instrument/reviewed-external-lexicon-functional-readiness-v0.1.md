# Reviewed External Lexicon Functional Readiness v0.1

Status: IMPLEMENTED_CONTRACT.

Project lane: Open Instrument / ZËRO.

## Purpose

This contract separates bounded functional lexical readiness from
historical-origin authority.

The instrument may evaluate whether an isolated form is lexically attested
and functionally relevant without claiming that the form is the historical
origin of another word.

## Functional lexical readiness supports

A passing row may support only:

- the isolated form is attested in the recorded language or dialect;
- the reviewed citation supplies a compatible ordinary lexical gloss;
- the form may be evaluated as a functional candidate;
- a bounded semantic bridge may be shown;
- the user decides.

## Functional lexical readiness does not support

A passing row does not support:

- historical derivation;
- borrowing direction;
- earliest origin;
- exclusive linguistic ownership;
- language superiority;
- a single historical winner;
- publication-grade historical proof;
- automatic candidate truth.

## DI posture

For the reviewed Albanian DI row:

- `di` is treated as a reviewed lexical form associated with knowing;
- DI may pass bounded functional lexical readiness;
- historical origin remains `not_claimed`;
- historical transmission remains unclaimed;
- candidate truth remains unclaimed;
- user decision posture remains `user_decides`.

## Relationship to production promotion

Functional readiness does not silently replace the existing production
promotion checklist.

Runtime projection requires a separate explicit authorization lane.

This contract exists so production governance does not redefine an ordinary
lexical claim as a historical-origin claim.

## Forbidden shortcuts

Do not:

- invent a DPEWA lemma locator;
- claim an unverified DPEWA entry;
- reuse DA citation metadata for DI;
- label a bridge source as a direct primary source;
- promote DI to historical truth;
- declare DI the historical source of `study`;
- bypass derivative or homophone protections.

## Current result

DI may pass functional lexical readiness while remaining outside production
runtime projection until a separate runtime authorization is approved.
