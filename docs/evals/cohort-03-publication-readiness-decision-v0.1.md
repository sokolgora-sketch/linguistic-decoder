Status: publication-readiness decision

## 1. Decision

Cohort 03 is not ready for public publication yet.

Current status:

> Cohort 03 is ready as an internal mechanism archive / research boundary, but not as a public paper, Zenodo archive, LingBuzz preprint, or README publication update.

This document records why publication is blocked and what would be required before a public Cohort 03 release.

Cohort 03 now contains substantial repo-tracked evidence, including:

- Finnish Phase A bridge results;
- Semitic Phase B Arabic/Hebrew `/a/` and `/i/` results;

- Hindi `/i/` mechanism synthesis.

However, the evidence is mechanism-rich but not publication-stable.

The strongest current Cohort 03 outputs are cautious mechanism boundaries, not clean cohort-level support claims.

## 4. Current evidence map

### 4.1 Finnish Phase A

Recorded summary:

| Case | Candidate bracket | Control bracket | Summary classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Finnish `/ä/` | `V1-V3` | `V2-V3` | clean lower/open-front `V1-V3` bridge support |
| Finnish `/ö/` | `V1-V3` | `V2-V5` | weaker lower front-rounded `V1-V3` bridge support |
| Finnish `/y/` | `V5-V7` | `V2-V5` | high-region pressure / anchor-instability; no `V5-V7` support |

Publication reading:

> Finnish Phase A is useful, but not uniformly clean. `/ä/` and `/ö/` are stronger than `/y/`, and `/y/` remains a high-region pressure case.

### 4.2 Semitic Phase B

Recorded Semitic Phase B evidence includes Arabic/Hebrew `/a/` and `/i/`.

Current reading:

- Arabic `/a/` shows weak / edge-stressed lower-open `V1-V3` directional support, not clean support.
- Hebrew `/a/` did not produce a clean stable `INTERMEDIATE` pattern.
- Arabic `/i/` and Hebrew `/i/` high-region tests returned high-collapse patterns, with no `INTERMEDIATE` rescue in those recorded packs.

Publication reading:

> Semitic Phase B is important pressure evidence, but not clean cohort-level support evidence.

### 4.3 Hindi `/i/`

Current synthesis classification:

> Hindi `/i/`: length-sensitive high-boundary pressure case.

Key Hindi evidence:

- Hindi `/i/` stayed `COLLAPSED_HIGH` under both `V5-V7` and `V6-V7` in the Hindi/Arabic comparison.
- Arabic `/i/` stabilized under `V6-V7`, so Hindi pressure is not explained by broad high-front placement alone.
- Replacing long-`ee` high anchors with short-`i` high anchors reduced pressure but did not fully rescue the original Hindi main target.
- Target/high-anchor shape mismatch, especially token length, is a major pressure source.
- Two independently curated length-matched Hindi `/i/` candidates returned `INTERMEDIATE`.
- The second length-matched replication candidate remained high-edge with `NEAR_COLLAPSE_HIGH` and `BOUNDARY_UNCERTAIN_HIGH`.

Publication reading:

> Hindi `/i/` has a strong mechanism story, but it is not a clean support case and should not be published as final evidence yet.

## 5. Why Cohort 03 is not publication-ready

Publication is blocked for these reasons:

1. Cohort 03 does not yet have a single clean cohort-level claim.
2. Several lanes are pressure / instability / edge-stress cases.
3. Hindi `/i/` is explicitly classified as length-sensitive and high-boundary stressed, not solved.
4. Finnish `/y/` remains a high-region pressure / anchor-instability case.
5. Semitic `/i/` tests remain collapsed rather than rescued.
6. Semitic `/a/` is weak / edge-stressed, not clean support.
7. There is no Cohort 03 public archive manifest.
8. There is no Cohort 03 checksum table.
9. There is no Cohort 03 reproduction runbook.
10. There is no Cohort 03 paper draft.
11. There is no Cohort 03 Zenodo package.
12. There is no Cohort 03 LingBuzz/preprint package.
13. README/publication claims have not been prepared or reviewed.
